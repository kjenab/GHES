We provide specialized services to support higher education institutions in recruiting, evaluating, developing, and retaining qualified faculty members and academic administrators. We also assist professionals seeking academic employment opportunities.

Our services include:

  • Assisting institutions with faculty and academic administrator recruitment and selection
  • Supporting the evaluation and matching of candidates with institutional needs
  • Providing academic leadership and faculty development support
  • Developing strategies for program effectiveness, growth, and faculty retention

For Employers / Institutions

If your institution is seeking qualified faculty members, academic administrators, or other higher education professionals, please email us the following:

  • Job title and complete job description
  • Required and preferred academic qualifications
  • Required teaching, research, administrative, or professional experience
  • Institution name and location
  • Expected starting date and any other important position requirements

Please send the information to:

ahmadzargari@global-higher-educational-services.com

For Employees / Candidates

If you are seeking an academic faculty, leadership, or administrative position, please email us:

  • Your updated Curriculum Vitae (CV)
  • A Statement of Purpose (SOP) describing your academic background, professional experience, areas of expertise, career goals, and the type of position you are seeking
  • Any additional information that may help us identify appropriate opportunities for you

Please send your documents to:

azargar@yahoo.com or ahmadzargari@global-higher-educational-services.com

Connecting Institutions with Qualified Candidates

After receiving information from employers and candidates, we review the requirements and qualifications to identify potential matches and facilitate communication between the parties.
